Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MUNAWAR DIGITAL ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit munawardigital.com or contact us.

Information We Collect

When you fill out our contact form, we collect the information you provide: your name, business name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, service of interest, budget range, and project details. We do not collect this information automatically — it is only sent when you submit the form, which opens WhatsApp with your message.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide only to respond to your inquiry and discuss your project. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

WhatsApp Communication

Our contact form and "Get a Free Consultation" buttons open WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. Messages sent this way are subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy, in addition to ours.

Cookies & Analytics

We may use Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to understand how visitors use this website and to measure the results of our advertising. These tools can set cookies and collect information such as pages visited, device type, approximate location and actions like clicking a WhatsApp button. They do not receive the details you type into our contact form. You can block these cookies in your browser settings.

Your Rights

You can request that we delete any information you've shared with us by contacting us at munawardigital@gmail.com.
